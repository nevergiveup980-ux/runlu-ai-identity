# RUNLU Project Map

The RUNLU ecosystem includes:

- **RUNLU publishing:** original fiction and multilingual literary work.
- **RUNLU ONE:** writing and document-production software; EPUB import/export capabilities are under development.
- **Flooring OS / Warehouse OS:** operational tools for flooring sales and warehouse workflows.
- **RUNLU SmartRoll™:** intelligent inventory roll selection and cut-planning functionality under development.
- **RUNLU STAGE:** planned AI-directed performance and video creation platform.
- **RUNLU games:** word and logic game experiments, including WORDSHIFT.
- **RUNLU website:** public editorial and product presence at https://runlu.ca.

This is a selective overview, not a statement that every project is released or commercially available. Verify live product status before making claims.
