# RUNLU · Long-Term Vision

**记录过去，服务现在，连接未来。**

RUNLU (润庐) is a long-term creative undertaking built around literature, practical software, thoughtful design, and human–AI collaboration.

## Guiding ideas

- Turn imaginative ideas into tangible, useful works.
- Build patiently, improve through evidence, and protect creative integrity.
- Let literature, technology, and design reinforce one another.
- Make works understandable and maintainable for future collaborators.
- Keep the human creator's intent, judgment, and authorship visible.

## Continuity

A good project can be understood beyond a single conversation or tool. Public documentation should describe the purpose and broad shape of each work, without exposing private working notes or credentials.

This is an evolving statement of intent, not a promise that every planned product has shipped.
