# Principles of Human–AI Collaboration

1. Human creative intent and final judgment remain central.
2. AI assists with research, drafting, engineering, editing, testing, and iteration.
3. Prefer verifiable outcomes over claims of completion.
4. Distinguish ideas, prototypes, development work, and released products.
5. Protect personal information, private business data, credentials, and unpublished work.
6. Treat this public profile as context, never as permission to act on accounts or publish materials.
